I believe good design is measured by who it doesn't leave behind. What started as a hobby turned into a career when I discovered how design can make things both look great and work better.

I focus on research-driven design- starting with real conversations, not assumptions. Every screen I design traces back to an actual user need, not a guess.

My process starts with listening, not sketching. Personas, interviews, and real pain points come before a single pixel because a beautiful screen solving the wrong problem still fails the user.
