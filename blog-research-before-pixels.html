Research before pixels: a simple 3-step interview script

A compact way to learn what users are trying to do before opening Figma.

Good interviews are less about clever questions and more about order. The goal is to understand the situation, the current behavior, and the friction before introducing any design solution.

Ask about the last real attempt

Start with a recent example. Specific memories reveal tools, workarounds, and emotional details that broad opinions often hide.

Map the steps out loud

Invite the user to walk through what happened from start to finish. Listen for delays, repeated checks, and moments where they needed help.

Save solution ideas for the end

Only after the problem is clear should you ask what they wish existed. By then, their suggestions are grounded in context instead of guesswork.
